## Description of Service

Route is an iOS app developed by Rinse Repeat Labs. It records drives you start and stop yourself, estimates the fuel cost of those drives, and shows the trade-off between travel time and fuel spend so you can make your own decision about how to drive.

## Estimates, not measurements

**Read this part.** It is the most important thing in this document.

Route does not measure fuel consumption. It cannot — a phone has no access to your vehicle's fuel system. Everything the App reports about fuel, cost, efficiency, savings, and range is an **estimate produced by a mathematical model** from your speed, your distance, the vehicle details you entered, and the fuel price you set.

Those estimates:

- **Are more reliable as comparisons than as absolute figures.** "80 mph costs about $12 more than 65 mph" is a far stronger claim than "this trip cost $73.17."
- **Do not model** acceleration, braking, hills, wind, payload, cold starts, air conditioning, traffic, construction, or rest stops.
- **Depend on what you told the App.** Speed limits are supplied by you and are never looked up. Route cannot verify them and will not check whether the limit you entered matches your actual route.
- **Are not a fuel gauge.** Range and fuel-stop figures in the App are planning estimates only. **Do not rely on Route to decide whether you can reach a fuel station.** Use your vehicle's own instruments.

Do not use Route where an inaccurate figure could cause harm or loss, including but not limited to fuel-critical route planning, commercial fleet billing, mileage reimbursement, tax reporting, or regulatory record-keeping.

## Safe and lawful driving

Route is designed to be reviewed **after** a drive, not read while moving.

- **Do not interact with the App while driving.** Set it before you go; look at it when you have stopped.
- **You are solely responsible for operating your vehicle safely and lawfully.** Nothing the App shows relieves you of that.
- **Route is not a speeding app.** It never recommends a speed above the posted limit you entered, and it never recommends a speed so far below surrounding traffic as to be unsafe. If any figure in the App appears to suggest otherwise, obey the law and the conditions on the road, not the App.
- **Obey all traffic laws, signage, and conditions**, which always take precedence over anything Route displays.
